# Privacy Policy — Chess

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This page tells you exactly how Chess handles your information. The answer is simple, and it does not change from game to game: nothing is collected.

## About this game

Chess is a classic board game for Apple devices (iOS). You play it on your own device, and it works offline. This policy applies to the Apple version of the game.

## In short

- Chess collects **no personal data at all**.
- There is **no account, no sign-in, and no login** of any kind.
- There is **no tracking**, **no third-party analytics**, and **no advertising** in the Apple version.
- Nothing you do in the game is sent to a server by this game.
- Your game progress, scores, and settings stay **on your device only**.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect, store, share, or sell any of the following, in the Apple version of Chess:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details
- Your location
- Your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
- Any device identifier used for tracking you across apps or websites
- Anything about how you browse the web or use other apps

Because none of this is collected, none of it can be shared with anyone else, and none of it can be sold. There is nothing to share and nothing to sell.

## What stays on your device only

The only things the game keeps are the things it needs to play on your own device, for example your game progress, your best scores, and your sound and display settings. These are stored on your device only, using the device's own storage. They are not sent anywhere by this game. If you delete the game from your device, that on-device information is deleted with it.

## Purchases

If Chess offers an in-app purchase on Apple, that purchase is handled entirely by Apple. This game does not receive, see, or store your payment details, card details, or Apple account details. A purchase only unlocks content inside the game itself; it does not change anything in this policy, and it does not cause any personal data to be collected.

## Children

Children and families: no personal information is collected from children either. The game has no chat, no accounts, and nothing a child could type in that would leave the device.

## Changes to this policy

That is the whole policy. If anything about it ever changes, the new version will be published at the same repository link before the change takes effect.
